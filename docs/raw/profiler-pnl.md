{% hint style="info" %}

### Data Freshness & Historical Coverage

For data freshness and historical coverage, see [Data Coverage by Chain](/api/data-coverage.md); this endpoint follows it except where noted below.

* The aggregate PnL summary is served from a **short server-side cache of up to about an hour**, so its figures may lag your most recent trades by up to that long; the detailed per-token PnL breakdown is served live within the standard latency.

> Calculate profit and loss metrics for a specific address and token. Provides detailed trading performance including realized gains from sales and unrealized gains from current holdings.\
> \
> What it helps to answer:\
> \
> \- Realized profits or losses from completed token sales\
> \- Unrealized gains or losses on current token holdings\
> \- Average purchase and sale prices for the token\
> \- Total return on investment

# Logo files

`chains/*.svg` and `tokens/*.svg` are copied unmodified from the npm package
`@web3icons/core@4.0.56` (https://web3icons.io), author: 0xa3k5,
license: MIT (as declared in its package.json).

They are trademarks of their respective projects and are shown only to
identify the chain or token next to Nansen data. `manifest.json` lists the
files; anything not listed falls back to a letter badge in the app.
Token logos not in this folder come from the `logo` URL the Nansen API
returns for that token, loaded directly by the viewer's browser.

# 07 · Editing plan

## Pacing

- Act 1 breathes (5 s), Acts 2–3 quicken (2.5–3.5 s shots, one 7 s hero), Act 4 slows for comprehension (9 s), Act 5 speeds up (2 s cuts), mobile is one continuous 8 s performance, the climax is two long, powerful shots.
- Cut frequency: about one cut every 3 s on average; never more than two cuts per bar.

## Camera

- Every desktop shot has a slow virtual camera: a push (1.00→1.10–1.35) or a drift, eased with a cubic in-out curve. No static frames longer than 1 s.
- The push always moves toward the element that carries the meaning (ring, verdict, count, answer).
- Reframes inside one shot (token: rings → chart; analyze: page → panel) use a 0.6 s eased move rather than a cut.
- Zoom never exceeds 1.35 on 1080p captures, to keep text sharp.

## Typography

- Manrope (the product's own UI face, instanced from the build's variable font), semibold, 44 px, white at 92% with a soft shadow, bottom-left at 96 px margins. Max 4 words. Fade and rise 12 px in 0.3 s, out in 0.25 s. At most one overlay on screen.

## Transitions

- Hard cuts on beats inside acts.
- One whip (0.2 s horizontal motion blur) from flows into the token page: the "zoom into one token" moment.
- Desktop→phone: the desktop frame scales to 0.62 and darkens while the phone rises from 20 px below into the centre of a dark stage with a soft green glow (0.5 s).
- Phone→desktop: hard cut on the downbeat at 0:50.
- Fades only at the very start (from black) and the end (to black, then the end card).

## Desktop to mobile

The phone sits on a near-black stage (#050807) with a faint mint radial glow, rounded 56 px corners, a 12 px dark bezel, and a soft shadow. Size: 990 px tall on the 1080 frame, so the glass and genie details stay readable. No fake hand or device photo.

## Overlays

Only the Peregrine mark (opening), 6 short captions and the end card. No feature lists, no formulas.

## Logo treatment

- **Opening:** the Peregrine mark at 220 px on black, scale 0.92→1.0 with a soft mint bloom, 1.5 s.
- **End card:** Nansen wordmark (or `assets/nansen-logo.png` if present), and below it "Powered by Nansen" in Manrope 32 px in the brand mint.

## Music sync

Cuts on beats at 0:06.5, 0:10.0, 0:12.5, 0:19.5, 0:22.5, 0:31.5, 0:34.0, 0:36.0, 0:42.0, 0:50.0, 0:54.0, 0:58.5. Drum drop-out during the AI question (0:24–0:28), full return as the answer streams. The phone section starts on the drop.

## Readability

- Never scale below 0.62 while text must be read; captions stay off data.
- Mild sharpening after scaling; full-range colour; no heavy grading (the product's colours are the brand).

## Avoiding chaos

One idea per shot, one overlay at a time, consistent easing, consistent direction of camera drift within an act, and silence of effects (only soft ticks) under dense screens.

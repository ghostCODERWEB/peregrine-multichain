# 04 · Story

One trader, one question at a time, one continuous workflow.

**Act 0 — Mark (0:00–0:01.5).** The Peregrine falcon mark resolves out of darkness. Silence into the first pad note.

**Act 1 — "What is the market doing?" (0:01.5–0:06.5).** The Overview answers in a sentence: *Accumulating Ton. Distributing Near.* The flow ring draws its arcs and particles start moving from the chains losing money to the chains gaining it. The viewer immediately sees depth: a live model of money moving across 27 chains.
*Why first:* it is the widest question and the product's strongest single frame.

**Act 2 — "What is moving?" (0:06.5–0:12.5).** From chains to tokens: the Alpha market map (150 tokens by flow and price), then Flow Index lines over seven days. The camera drifts across the map and settles on one token.
*Why next:* the viewer goes from "where" to "what", and the density proves breadth.

**Act 3 — "Is it safe?" (0:12.5–0:22.5).** The token page opens. The Token Score rings animate in: Nansen risk and Peregrine's model side by side. The chart switches range and style under the cursor. A cut to the rug check gives the blunt verdict: *Rug risk: High.*
*Why here:* a trader who found a token needs a judgement, and this is where the product's model plus Nansen shows most clearly.

**Act 4 — "Why?" (0:22.5–0:31.5).** ⌘J. Analyze with Nansen slides in. The user picks the score card on the page (it outlines itself), asks *"Why is the dump risk moderate, and who is selling?"*, and the answer streams in, citing the numbers on screen.
*Why here:* the verdict raised a question; the AI answers it, grounded in the page.

**Act 5 — "Who is behind it, and what else?" (0:31.5–0:42).** A glimpse of the wallet Profiler: a trader score ring and verdict for spot, perps and Polymarket. Then the Token Checker's cross-network table, and three fast market boards (predictions, perps, sectors) on the beat.
*Why here:* from token to people, then the scope of the product, building energy into the phone drop.

**Act 6 — "Take it with you" (0:42–0:50).** The last desktop board shrinks into the dark and a phone rises onto the stage. Today screen, a tab tap (the glass lens glides), a scroll (the bar shrinks, content moves under the glass), then the Ask button: the genie grows out of the tab bar and answers.
*Why here:* the same workflow in the pocket; the most delightful motion arrives when attention could dip.

**Act 7 — Climax (0:50–0:58.5).** Two fast, powerful reprises: the Proof page ("7,903 Nansen calls", ROC curves drawing) for trust, and a final push into the Overview flow ring as the music peaks.
*Why last:* end on credibility and the product's signature image.

**Act 8 — End card (0:58.5–1:00).** Nansen logo, "Powered by Nansen". The music resolves.
